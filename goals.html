Goals

Practical commitments for designing learning that is more meaningful, more inclusive, and more alive.

  • Design learning experiences that prioritize meaningful cognitive engagement over simple compliance.
  • Create environments where every learner experiences voice, choice, agency, and belonging.
  • Bridge research and practice by building tools educators can adapt, not simply adopt.
  • Model the curiosity, reflection, experimentation, and continuous learning I hope to inspire in others.
  • Share freely. Iterate often. Keep building.
